# 🇮🇳 ODOP in Northeast India — Power BI Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** analyzing the **One District One Product (ODOP)** initiative across Northeast India, with a focus on the geographical, sectoral, product-level, and GI-related characteristics of ODOP products.

The dashboard converts ODOP data into interactive visualizations that allow users to explore products across states and districts and understand their distribution across different economic sectors and categories.

---

 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is organized into four analytical sections:

### 1. Overview

Provides a high-level summary of ODOP across Northeast India, including:

* Total ODOP records
* States covered
* Districts covered
* ODOP records by state
* Economic-category classification
* Sector composition

This page provides an overall picture of the ODOP landscape before moving into detailed analysis.

---

### 2. Geographic & District Explorer

Allows users to explore the geographical distribution of ODOP products.

**Interactive features include:**

* State filter
* District filter
* ODOP Product filter
* District-level geographic visualization
* District-level ODOP details
* Distinct district count
* Distinct ODOP product count

Users can select a state or district to examine the corresponding ODOP products and characteristics.

---

### 3. Sector & Product Analysis

Examines the composition and distribution of ODOP products across different sectors and categories.

**Visualizations include:**

* ODOP Sector Composition
* ODOP Product Category Analysis
* Top 10 ODOP Products
* Sector × Product Category analysis

This section helps identify the concentration of ODOP products across sectors and product categories.

---

### 4. GI & Product Geography

Explores the relationship between ODOP products and **Geographical Indication (GI) status**.

**Visualizations include:**

* GI Status Composition
* GI Product Geography
* GI Products by State

The geographic visualization allows users to examine the distribution of products according to their GI status.

---

## 🗺️ Geographic Coverage

The dashboard focuses on the **Northeastern region of India** and examines ODOP products at the **state and district levels**.

The analysis considers dimensions such as:

* State
* District
* ODOP Product
* Sector
* Economic Category
* Product Category
* GI Status

## 📈 Key Analytical Dimensions

The dashboard analyzes ODOP records across several dimensions:

| Dimension         | Purpose                                         |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| State             | Compare ODOP distribution across states         |
| District          | Examine district-level product distribution     |
| ODOP Product      | Explore individual products                     |
| Sector            | Analyze broad sectoral composition              |
| Economic Category | Examine product classification                  |
| Product Category  | Compare different product categories            |
| GI Status         | Explore Geographical Indication characteristics |
